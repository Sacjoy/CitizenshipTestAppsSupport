Before you download

What is the mock-test format?

24 questions in 45 minutes. Pass with at least 18 correct answers. This is the app's default mock format. Practice results do not guarantee an official test pass.

Does the app include audio or translations?

No built-in audio lessons, spoken question narration or translated study content are included. The app's study content is in English.

Can I study without an internet connection?

The app stores bundled questions and downloaded question data on your device. An internet connection is needed for purchases, Restore Purchases and new content downloads. We do not promise that every feature will work offline. Before travelling, open the app online and check that the study content you need is available.

Do I need an account?

No app account or sign-in is required. Apple handles the optional Lifetime Access purchase.

Try three sample practice questions

These examples come from the app's bundled practice bank. They are independently prepared study questions, not actual official examination questions. The answers below were checked against the linked government sources on 10 November 2026. This check covers these three samples, not the entire paid question bank.

1. Which principle is considered a fundamental value of life in the UK?

  1. Democracy
  2. Rule by the military
  3. One-party government
  4. Hereditary voting rights
Show answer and explanation

Democracy. Democracy is one of the fundamental principles of life in the UK. Check the official source.

2. What does the rule of law mean?

  1. Everyone is subject to the law
  2. Only citizens must obey the law
  3. Parliament can ignore the law
  4. Judges may choose which laws apply
Show answer and explanation

Everyone is subject to the law. The rule of law means that the law applies to everyone. Check the official source.

3. Which statement best reflects individual liberty in the UK?

  1. People may make lawful choices about how they live
  2. People may ignore laws they dislike
  3. Only elected officials have personal freedoms
  4. Religious belief is compulsory
Show answer and explanation

People may make lawful choices about how they live. Individual liberty includes the freedom to make your own lawful choices. Check the official source.
