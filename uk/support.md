# UK Citizenship Test Support

Support for our independent Life in the UK Test preparation app.

## Contact Support

For help with Lifetime Access, restoring a purchase, practice questions, mock tests, the study guide, question-bank updates, or another app issue, email:

**[mohongandhi5@gmail.com](mailto:mohongandhi5@gmail.com)**

Please include **UK Citizenship Test** in the subject line, your iPhone or iPad model, iOS/iPadOS version, and a short description of the issue.

Screenshots can help us diagnose problems. Please remove unnecessary personal information before sending them. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, Apple Account credentials, passports, immigration documents, or full purchase tokens.

## Lifetime Access and Purchases

Lifetime Access is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple.

If you already purchased Lifetime Access, open the app and use **Restore Purchases**.

We do not receive or store your payment-card details. Purchase verification may require an internet connection.

If a purchase was charged by Apple but premium access does not appear, first try **Restore Purchases**. If the issue continues, contact us using the email above and describe what happened. Do not send your Apple Account password or payment details.

## Premium Question Bank

Premium question-bank updates are delivered securely after an eligible Lifetime Access purchase is verified.

If premium questions do not load:

1. Confirm the device has an internet connection.
2. Reopen the app.
3. Use **Restore Purchases** if Lifetime Access is not showing as active.
4. Contact support if the issue continues.

## Practice Questions, Mock Tests and Study Guide

The app includes Life in the UK Test preparation features such as practice questions, mock tests, saved questions, mistake review, progress tracking and study material.

Practice scores and readiness indicators are learning tools only. They do not guarantee a pass in the official Life in the UK Test.
