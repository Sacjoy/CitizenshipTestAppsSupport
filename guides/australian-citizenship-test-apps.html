What matters when choosing a citizenship app?

  • Content you can check. Use the official Australian Citizenship: Our Common Bond material to verify the subjects and answers you study. A large question count alone does not establish accuracy.
  • Useful mistake review. Choose tools that help you revisit weak topics, rather than only repeat a score.
  • Clear access limits. Check which mocks, practice questions, explanations and study material are available before payment.
  • A payment model that suits you. Compare free, subscription and lifetime options, and read the actual purchase screen.
  • The format you need. Consider English reading, listening support, device compatibility and internet access before choosing.

Start with the official study resources

The Department of Home Affairs directs learners to Australian Citizenship: Our Common Bond. Its preparation page also links to a podcast, a sample practice test and AMEPOnline citizenship modules. The booklet is available in community languages; the test is conducted in English.

Use the official material to check answers about Australia's people, democratic beliefs, government, law and values. The government sample test helps you understand the format; its questions will differ from those on test day. Home Affairs does not endorse paid external preparation apps.

A practical study routine

  1. Read a topic. After a section of Our Common Bond, explain the main idea in your own words.
  2. Try a mock. Record the topics behind incorrect answers and answers you guessed.
  3. Revisit weak areas. Check those sections against the official booklet, then use topic practice and mistake review if your chosen app offers them.
  4. Check understanding. Work through unfamiliar phrases slowly. Memorising a sequence of answers can hide gaps in comprehension.

This is a suggested revision routine, not a validated pass predictor.
