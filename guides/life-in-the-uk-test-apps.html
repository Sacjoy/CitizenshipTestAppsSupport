Start with the official study resources

GOV.UK's Life in the UK Test page says the test is based on the official Guide for New Residents. It links to the official shop for books, electronic and audio formats, e-learning and the official practice app. The test has 24 questions and a 45-minute limit.

Keep study purchases separate from test booking. An app download does not book a test. Use GOV.UK for the official booking route and current requirements.

A practical study routine

  1. Read before drilling questions. Work through the official handbook section by section, making short notes in your own words.
  2. Organise confusing facts. Use a timeline for historical events and a separate set of notes for institutions and customs. Check each note against the handbook.
  3. Practise with the time limit. Once you have studied the material, try a 24-question mock within 45 minutes. Note which questions took the most thought as well as those you missed.
  4. Review the reason behind an answer. Explain why the other choices are wrong. Return to the relevant handbook section rather than immediately repeating the same mock.

A high score on a familiar question set is only one signal. Check whether you can recall and explain the material without the answer choices.

Questions to ask before choosing

Is UK Citizenship Test 2027 the official Life in the UK app?

No. Our app is an independent preparation product. The TSO app in the comparison is the official practice app. Our questions are independently prepared, and our app is not endorsed by the Home Office or the publishers of the official learning materials.

Does a larger question bank prove better preparation?

No. A question count does not measure accuracy, coverage or your understanding. Look for clear explanations, check material against the official source and revisit weak topics. No app can guarantee your result.
