What does Lifetime Access include?

Each app has its own optional one-time purchase. Lifetime Access unlocks that app's:

  • 2,500 practice questions with topic-based and mixed practice.
  • Full study guide and unlimited mock tests.
  • Saved questions, mistake review, progress tracking and mock test history.
  • Future question-bank updates.

The free option includes two mock tests per app. Purchases are processed by Apple. To recover an existing purchase, use Restore Purchases in the relevant app with the same Apple Account.

Independent study tools

These are independent educational apps, not government products or official examination questions. They are not affiliated with or endorsed by the Australian, Canadian or UK governments. Practice scores do not guarantee a pass.
