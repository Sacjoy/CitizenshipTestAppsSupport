Publisher disclosure

This page is published by the developer, Megha Sachin Jadhav. It is factual background for readers, educators and reviewers, not an independent review. Our three country apps are separate, unofficial educational products. We benefit when someone purchases Lifetime Access.

The offer

Each app provides two free mock tests with answer and result review. A one-time Lifetime Access purchase unlocks 2,500 practice questions, the full study guide, unlimited mock tests, saved questions, mistake review, progress tracking and future question-bank updates. There is no recurring subscription and no app account is required. Practice Questions and the full Study Guide require purchase. Prices vary by storefront; use the current App Store purchase screen.

Limitations to include in a review

  • English study content. No built-in audio lessons, question narration or translations.
  • Bundled and downloaded question data is stored on the device. Purchases, restore and new downloads require internet. Full offline functionality is not promised.
  • Each country's Lifetime Access purchase applies to that app only.
  • Practice questions are independently prepared, not official examination questions. Neither question count nor practice scores guarantee passing.
  • Our website comparisons describe public listings. They are disclosed publisher comparisons, not hands-on independent reviews.

Official study sources

  • Australia: Home Affairs and Our Common Bond
  • Canada: Discover Canada
  • United Kingdom: GOV.UK Life in the UK Test

How to evaluate the apps

Start with the two free mocks. Check question wording and explanations against the official study source, inspect the timer and results, and check what is locked before purchase. The screenshots linked above show our published App Store interface. The nine public sample answers were source-checked on 10 November 2026; that limited check is not an audit of all 2,500 questions in each app.

Download-link measurement

Our App Store links include Apple campaign labels so we can understand which pages lead to downloads. When the browser supplies a recognised AI referral or source tag, the label identifies that source. This does not identify individual readers, and our attribution script uses no cookies or local storage. A missing referrer stays unclassified.
