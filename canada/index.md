# Privacy Policy for Canada Citizenship Test

**Last updated: September 15, 2026**

Canada Citizenship Test is designed to help users prepare for the Canadian citizenship test. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app stores, what information may be transmitted, and how third-party services are used.

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect

Canada Citizenship Test does not require you to create an account.

The app does not intentionally collect your:

* name
* email address
* phone number
* home address
* precise location
* contacts
* photos
* advertising identifier
* payment-card information

The app does not contain advertising, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking technology.

## 2. Study Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores study-related information locally on your device so that your progress can be remembered.

This may include:

* practice and topic-test statistics
* saved question identifiers
* incorrectly answered question identifiers
* mock-test usage
* mock-test scores and history
* pass/fail results
* app appearance preferences
* premium-access status
* downloaded question-bank version information

Mock-test history may include information such as the date of a test, score, number of questions and whether the test was passed.

This study information is not intentionally transmitted to our servers.

You can reset certain study data using the controls available within the app.

## 3. In-App Purchases

Lifetime premium access is purchased through Apple's App Store using StoreKit.

Apple processes the payment. Canada Citizenship Test does not receive or store your payment-card details.

To verify eligibility for premium content, the app may securely transmit the signed StoreKit transaction provided by Apple to our backend service.

The transaction is used to:

* verify that the purchase belongs to the Canada Citizenship Test app
* verify the premium product purchased
* confirm that the purchase is valid
* detect revoked or refunded purchases
* authorize access to premium question-bank content

Our application backend does not intentionally store the signed transaction in an application database after verification.

Apple may separately process and retain purchase information under Apple's own privacy policies and App Store terms.

Apple Privacy Policy:
https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## 4. Premium Question-Bank Downloads

Premium question-bank content is delivered using Supabase.

When an eligible purchase is successfully verified, our backend creates a short-lived signed download link that allows the app to download the private premium question bank.

The signed download link currently expires after approximately 60 seconds.

The downloaded question bank is stored within the app's local storage on your device.

Our Supabase project for the Canada app is configured in the Canada region.

## 5. Network Requests

The app may connect to our backend infrastructure to:

* retrieve app configuration
* check for updated question-bank content
* verify eligibility for premium content
* download question-bank content

These requests are not designed to transmit your study history, saved questions, test scores or mock-test results.

Like most internet services, hosting and infrastructure providers may automatically process technical information associated with network requests, such as IP addresses, timestamps, request metadata and diagnostic information.

## 6. Third-Party Services

Canada Citizenship Test currently uses the following external services:

### Apple

Apple provides:

* App Store distribution
* StoreKit in-app purchasing
* purchase and entitlement information

Apple processes information according to its own privacy policies.

### Supabase

Supabase provides backend and storage infrastructure used for configuration, purchase verification and secure question-bank delivery.

Supabase may process technical request information as part of providing its infrastructure services.

Supabase Privacy Policy:
https://supabase.com/privacy

### Government of Canada Websites

The app may provide links to official Government of Canada resources. If you choose to open one of these links, your use of that website is governed by the website's own terms and privacy practices.

Canada Citizenship Test is an independent study application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the Government of Canada.

## 7. Advertising and Tracking

Canada Citizenship Test does not currently contain third-party advertising.

We do not use your activity in the app for cross-app or cross-website tracking.

We do not sell your personal information.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Canada Citizenship Test is designed for people preparing for the Canadian citizenship test and is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly operate a system for collecting personal information from children.

## 9. Data Retention and Deletion

Study progress and history stored by the app remain on your device until they are reset through the app, removed as part of normal app/device management, or the app is deleted, subject to Apple's device and backup behaviour.

Because the app does not require an account, we do not maintain a user-profile database that you need to ask us to delete.

Infrastructure providers such as Apple and Supabase may retain technical or transaction-related information according to their own policies and legal obligations.

## 10. Security

We use reasonable technical measures intended to protect premium content and purchase verification.

These measures include:

* Apple's signed StoreKit transactions
* server-side transaction verification
* private question-bank storage
* short-lived signed download links
* encrypted HTTPS connections

No internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app's features, data practices, service providers or legal requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
