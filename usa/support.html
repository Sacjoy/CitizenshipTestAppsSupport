Contact support

Restore Lifetime Access

If you already purchased Lifetime Access and see a locked screen, use the same Apple Account you used to buy it, then choose Restore Purchases in the app. Lifetime Access is a one-time purchase. If it still appears locked, email us before purchasing again.

Language and audio practice

Choose English or Español when starting a full interview. N-400 and civics practice support both languages. English reading and writing exercises stay in English.

Tap Play to hear a question. If you hear nothing, check your media volume and whether sound is playing through connected headphones.
