Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026 · Version 1.0

Your study information stays on your device

The app does not require an account. Your test profile, settings, practice history and progress are stored locally. We do not receive your filing date or answers to N-400 practice questions. The app does not use advertising or third-party analytics and does not track you across apps or websites.

Microphone and speech recognition

If you choose to answer aloud, the app asks for permission and uses on-device speech recognition on supported devices. It does not send us your recordings or transcripts. You can revoke microphone and speech recognition permissions in your device settings.

Purchases and your controls

Apple processes Lifetime Access purchases and restores through the App Store. We do not receive your payment card details. In the app settings you can reset learning progress or clear your test profile. Resetting progress does not cancel your purchase or reset the trial allowance.

Contacting us

If you voluntarily email support, we receive the email address and information you choose to send. We use this correspondence to answer and resolve your request. You can ask us to delete your correspondence. Please do not send immigration documents or personal N-400 answers.
